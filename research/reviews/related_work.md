## Defensible research positioning

The defensible question is: **Can a cheap model recover an independently supported audit conclusion and applicable standards evidence under a constrained acquisition budget as reliably as one frontier comparator?** Answering it requires repairing benchmark validity before optimizing citation exact match. A mislabeled paragraph cannot support a performance claim even when every model reproduces it.

Do not claim the first financial evidence graph, multi-agent auditing system, deterministic financial verifier, fixed tool-step budget, synthetic binder generator or financial reasoning benchmark with supporting facts. All have clear predecessors in the sources above. A candidate contribution is the *combination* of controlled incomplete evidence, heterogeneous acquisition costs, balanced clean/insufficient-evidence controls, independently adjudicated sufficient proof sets, and paragraph applicability. Novelty remains a hypothesis until a broader systematic review establishes the nearest competing work.

The narrow initial scope should be **evidence-supported financial reporting verification and citation applicability**, not proving fraud. Public financial statements generally lack the invoice, shipment, contract, side-letter and confirmation evidence needed for recognition judgments or fraudulent-intent labels. Synthetic additions must be clearly marked and reviewed; SEC totals do not validate invented transaction narratives.

For every scored case, record a dated accounting issue/assertion, applicable standard version, evidence spans, the rationale connecting them, and acceptable citation alternatives. Distinguish a taxonomy reference's association with a concept from whether a paragraph governs the observed error. A current-asset definition cannot be accepted for an unrelated equity misstatement merely because the concept map returned it. Permit `insufficient_evidence` and `no_applicable_paragraph_in_corpus` without scoring either as silent correctness.

For process scoring, compare recovered sufficient proof sets and missing/contradictory evidence rather than matching an arbitrary single trajectory or hidden edge ordering. Alternative valid investigations must be allowed. Evidence acquisition costs are simulated study design values; model/API costs are actual measured expenditures. Report both separately.

The same blind test cases, visible starting evidence, retrieval corpus, tools and budgets must be used for every method. Start with constant-label, statement/type-only, arithmetic-only, oracle-evidence and random-acquisition controls; then run direct prompting, retrieved candidate selection, candidate entailment validation and budgeted acquisition ablations. Use exactly one frontier family and cheap/open-weight alternatives. A literature table, a frontier-generated label, or a model judging its own output cannot establish a matched frontier win.

Five to ten companies are acceptable for a budget-limited **pilot**, but do not guarantee representation or strong confidence intervals. Split by company and filing/event family before case augmentation; balance controls within rule/statement strata; report company-macro results, paired group-level differences, case counts and sensitivity to each company. With very few clusters, bootstrap intervals can be unstable: disclose that limit and report all cluster outcomes. Pre-register primary metrics and stop rules before inspecting the final test.

## License and provenance checks

The catalog keeps paper rights, dataset rights and code licenses separate. FinQA, ConvFinQA and MultiHiertt repositories expose MIT licenses. EDINET-Bench code is Apache-2.0 but its data card states PDL-1.0 under EDINET terms. FinMR and the FinReason hidden-phase card state CC-BY-4.0. FinancialAuditBench code and card state MIT. FinanceBench's public subset and FinReflectKG are **CC-BY-NC-4.0**. A noncommercial corpus must not silently inherit a new permissive code license.

AuditBench/FinAuditing linked repository license requests returned 404; that records a failed verification, not proof of no license. No AuditFlow/AuditFraudBench code or dataset license was verified from the supplied pages. Check each upstream source independently before redistribution. In particular, publicly searchable ASC/IFRS paragraph identifiers and references do not establish permission to publish complete standards text. Release metadata, original annotations, pointers and appropriately licensed code; keep restricted excerpts separate until rights are established.
