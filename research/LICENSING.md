the Python research modules and scripts, the original dashboard builder/interface,
and the new research regression tests under `tests/`.

data/results/docs, third-party model catalogue entries, SEC filing contents,

external datasets. The new synthetic fixture generator is licensed software;
its original generated fixture records are CC-BY-4.0 under
`artifacts/SYNTHETIC_DATA_LICENSE.md`. The current fixture is unvalidated
development data, not an accountant-reviewed benchmark.

this code license grants rights in underlying sources. Resolve dataset and
inherited contributor permissions before packaging a fully licensed benchmark.
